# V3 Research Protocol

## Research Before Interpretation
Discover the actual URL inventory rather than requesting a fixed list alone.
Sample across offer, use case, commercial terms, proof, documentation/trust and entity pages.
A crawl page budget is not a link depth, and sampled pages are not a full-site crawl.

The portable crawler follows sitemap indexes with a bounded sitemap budget, stratifies discovered URLs and records unvisited URLs/failures.
It reads HTML with Cheerio and XML with fast-xml-parser. JavaScript-blocked pages may use a labelled public reader proxy; that is NOT browser-rendered evidence.
When no owned-page business body can be retrieved, stop and ask for accessible pages or a site export.

Each source has a stable ID from its URL (or query + URL for search snippets), publisher, type, retrieval time, excerpt and content hash.
The runtime records content changes; a cached source must never masquerade as a fresh fetch.
Do not treat pages sharing a publisher or syndicated text as independent demand evidence.

## Two Business Passes
Pass one proposes current offer, buyer, job, constraints, market assignments and disconfirming queries.
Pass two sees newly retrieved category/external evidence and tests those hypotheses.
There is no default preference for SaaS, procurement, a single SKU or a bundled workflow.
Client strategy is attributed context, not independent evidence or permission to invent product capabilities.
An unclear payer/current offer returns needs_confirmation and stops final generation.

## Demand And Competitors
Use brand identity queries plus category, problem, price, risk, implementation, community and alternative language.
Search should follow the material business lines and target locale.
Read pages for providers and important decision evidence. Snippets alone cannot prove a competitor serves a feature.
A publisher naming several products is a source competitor, not each named provider's verified capability.
Classify same-L3, adjacent-L3, substitute, source-only and unresolved separately.

Do not exclude category demand because the brand cannot serve it.
Do not mark unsupported industry demand as a target capability.
Keep exclusions, failed retrievals and unsearched query backlog explicit.

## Scope And Stop Conditions
Enumerate material semantic decisions before Topic grouping.
Run an independent gap review using original sources and the intent ontology.
Stop after bounded repair/research rounds; retain unresolved concerns when new gaps continue to appear.
Return coverage of the known registry, never an internet-wide completeness percentage.
If the model/request budget ends before the pipeline completes, fail clearly rather than serving a successful partial template.

The default budgets are implementation safety controls. Adjust them to evidence breadth and cost, not to produce a desired topic count.
The runtime currently holds an active job in the request; disconnect/cancellation is not durable resumability.
